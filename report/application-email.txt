Subject: Prototyping Engineer application — digital enclosure design study

Hi comma team,

I developed a digital mechanical-design study of a compact electronics enclosure, focusing on packaging, thermal contact and manufacturing decisions. The removable aluminum lid also acts as the heat sink.

The work includes editable CAD, nominal fit checks, a thermal-pad tolerance stack, and a fin comparison constrained by enclosure height and cutter access. A useful finding is that the assumed cooling resistance has not been justified by geometry alone; the study now quantifies the effective heat transfer the lid would need.

The repository starts with a plain-language design guide and includes an eight-page report, drawings and a proposed CNC sequence. This is a digital study developed with AI assistance; I have not fabricated or physically validated it. I would be glad to discuss the assumptions, design choices and calculations in detail.
