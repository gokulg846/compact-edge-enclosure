Subject: Prototyping Engineer application — compact hardware enclosure study

Hi comma team,

I built an independent compact edge-compute enclosure study to demonstrate how I approach packaging, manufacturing and validation. The repository includes editable parametric CAD, reimport-checked STEP solids, watertight STL exports, a CNC setup plan, dimensioned drawings, a 3-2-1 inspection fixture and rerunnable thermal/geometry checks.

The useful result is a tradeoff: the assumed passive configuration misses the thermal screening target, while forced cooling warrants a physical test. I also documented the thermal-pad tolerance stack and the remaining compression-force risk.

This is a digital design and manufacturing-planning project, not a claim of completed machining or physical thermal testing. It builds on my mechanical-design experience and Purdue Master of Engineering Management; I would be glad to walk through the design decisions and first-article validation plan.
